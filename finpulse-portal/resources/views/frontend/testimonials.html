Content policy

Why the demo labels testimonials so heavily

Fake customer quotes are a common and damaging pattern in financial marketing. A design demonstration should model the opposite habit.

  • Clear placement The notice appears above the quotes, not in a footnote.
  • Names that cannot be mistaken for people "Sample Merchant A" is intentionally not a realistic full name.
  • No performance claims No quote claims a specific interest rate, approval or financial result.
  • Illustrated avatars Simple SVG avatars avoid implying that a real photograph exists.

If this were a real deployment

Real testimonials would need documented consent, an accurate description of the person's relationship with the business, and a clear statement of whether they were compensated. Financial promotions are regulated in most markets.

Do not reuse these quotes

Because the people are fictional, reusing this text as marketing for a real product would be misleading. It exists only to demonstrate layout.
