Eligibility criteria (examples)

What a lender might ask to see

These are examples of the information a real lender could request. They are not requirements of the demonstration, and passing them would not produce an approval.

  • Trading history Often six to twelve months of records showing what the business actually earns.
  • Business identification Trade licence, registration or a local equivalent, depending on the jurisdiction.
  • Cash-flow evidence Bank statements, mobile wallet statements or a simple sales ledger.
  • Owner identification Standard identity documents, verified by the provider rather than by this demo.
  • Security or guarantee, where applicable Some products require an asset or a personal guarantee; terms always vary.

Responsible lending

Questions worth asking before signing

What is the total cost?

Add every instalment across the whole term, then add every fee. That total — not the monthly figure — is what the loan costs.

What happens if I am late?

Late fees, penalty interest and reporting consequences should be explained before you sign, not after a missed payment.

Can I repay early?

Ask whether early repayment reduces interest and whether a settlement fee applies.

Does the instalment fit the worst week?

Test the instalment against your quietest trading week, not your best one.
