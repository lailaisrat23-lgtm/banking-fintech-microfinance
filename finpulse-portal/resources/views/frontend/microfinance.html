Service overview

Designed for businesses that repay as they trade

In a market where a shop's daily takings are small and frequent, a monthly instalment can feel like a large cliff edge. Microfinance generally works by matching repayment timing to income rhythm.

The demonstration never claims that a person is eligible, and never promises approval. It shows the shape of a conversation: how much, how often, for how long, and what it costs in total.

Market and street vendors

Daily cash income, small stock purchases and little formal paperwork.

Agro-processing households

Seasonal income with a clear peak and a long quiet period.

Home-based producers

Tailoring, food preparation and craft work run from a household.

Micro-distributors

Small routes that need stock upfront and collect payment across the week.

Repair and maintenance

Skilled service work with uneven weekly demand.

First-time borrowers

People who have never held a formal loan and need the basics explained.

Application process

Six steps, no surprises

  1. 1

    Discuss the purpose

    What the money is for, and how it generates the income to repay it.

  2. 2

    Review affordability

    Compare the instalment with a conservative estimate of weekly income.

  3. 3

    Agree amount and term

    Test two or three structures with the calculator before choosing one.

  4. 4

    Understand total cost

    Look at interest and any charges across the whole term, not only the instalment.

  5. 5

    Complete documentation

    Sample checklists make the required records visible before signing anything.

  6. 6

    Plan the repayment routine

    Decide which day the instalment is set aside, and how a slow week is handled.

Responsible borrowing guidance

Borrow for a purpose that produces income. Keep an instalment well below the smallest realistic week, so an ordinary bad week does not become a missed payment.

Transparency principles

  • Total repayment is stated, not only the instalment
  • Fees and charges are listed separately from interest
  • Late-payment consequences are explained in advance
  • Borrowers can ask for the schedule in writing

No promises

This demonstration does not promise a loan, an approval or a specific rate. Any real application would be assessed by a licensed provider under its own rules.
