  • No payment can be made, collected, refunded or settled.
  • No investment can be made, and no marketplace opportunity is real.
  • No account can be opened and no funds can be held.
  • No licence, registration or regulatory approval is claimed or implied.

3. Estimates and illustrative figures

Balances, transactions, loan products, repayment schedules, funding progress, testimonials and statistics on this site are fictional sample data. The loan calculator performs a real calculation on the values you enter, but applies a simplified model and excludes fees, taxes, insurance and other charges. Its results are estimates for learning purposes only and are not quotations.

4. Acceptable use

  • Present the fictional brand, testimonials or figures as real.

  • Enter real financial credentials or another person's personal data into any demonstration field.

The demonstration is provided "as is". No warranty is given about accuracy, completeness, availability or fitness for any purpose. Educational content is general information and does not replace advice from a qualified financial, legal or tax professional.

Because the site provides no financial service and processes no data, no liability is accepted for any decision taken on the basis of its illustrative content. This clause is sample wording and would require review before any real use.
