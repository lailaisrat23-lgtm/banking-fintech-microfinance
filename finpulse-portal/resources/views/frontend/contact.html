    What happens after you submit

    • Your entries are checked against simple browser rules.
    • Any problem is listed in a summary and beside the field.
    • The form then shows a demonstration confirmation.
    • Nothing is transmitted, stored, emailed or ticketed.

    Client-side validation is not security

    Browser validation is a convenience that helps people avoid mistakes. It cannot protect a server, and this demonstration has no server at all. Real systems need server-side verification, rate limiting and secure storage.
