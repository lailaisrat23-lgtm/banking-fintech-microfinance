Overview

From a customer's phone to a readable statement

A merchant payment has three separate moments: the customer authorises, the payment clears, and the money settles into the business account. Small businesses often see only the middle step and then guess about the rest.

The demonstration below keeps those moments visually distinct and labels each one in words. Colour is used as a secondary cue only; every status also carries a text label such as Completed, Pending or Failed.

  • Text-first statuses. Customers and staff do not need to interpret colour to understand a payment.
  • Plain settlement language. Gross amount, fee and expected date are shown as separate, labelled figures.
  • Reconciliation by exception. Matched items stay quiet; only unmatched items are called out.

Reconciliation concept

Reconciliation compares what the business recorded against what the payment records show. The demonstration highlights only exceptions, because a long list of matched items hides the two that need attention.

  • Matched automatically Sample orders with a matching amount and reference.
  • Needs review — amount differs One sample order recorded BDT 1,240; the payment was BDT 1,204.
  • Needs review — missing payment A sample order exists with no corresponding payment record.
